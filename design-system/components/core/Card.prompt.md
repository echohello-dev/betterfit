Groups a related decision or set of information on a flat solid surface.

```jsx
<Card>
  <div className="bf-label">Today</div>
  <div className="bf-display" style={{ fontSize: 'var(--text-title-1)' }}>Push A</div>
</Card>
```

Tones: `surface` (default), `raised` (nested wells, segmented backgrounds), `outline`, `identity` (yellow brand field). A card must earn its container — content that could sit directly on the page should.
